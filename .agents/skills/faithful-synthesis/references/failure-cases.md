# Known failures and what to inspect

These examples are evaluation aids, not fixed writing templates or conclusions to apply to every document.

## Accurate fragments, misleading whole
Source: method A reduces cost under X but lowers accuracy; use depends on tolerable accuracy loss.
Failure: 'A is an effective cost-saving method.'
Inspect: restore X, loss, and the decision tradeoff. A correct cost claim alone is insufficient.

## Form mistaken for stage compliance
Instruction: discuss possible directions, ask questions, and stop before constructions or calculations.
Response: has the right headings and stops at section 6, but constructs a vector field and derives a threshold earlier.
Inspect: the prohibition applies throughout the stage. Ending location does not erase prohibited content.

## Optional path made mandatory
Source: paths that do not pass through multiplication are also allowed.
Failure: 'The required path must avoid multiplication.'
Inspect: preserve permissive wording; do not strengthen 'also allowed' to 'must'.

## Criticism ignoring another premise
Model has different input/output transforms; a separate condition constrains their relation.
Failure: declare a gap solely because transforms differ without checking that condition.
Inspect: all stated premises and their joint implications. Critical tone proves nothing.

## Arithmetic promoted to full correctness
A threshold calculation is checked in one chosen parametrization.
Failure: 'This proves a universal natural boundary.'
Inspect: distinguish arithmetic, parametrization, domain, model assumptions, and the generality of the conclusion.

## Research becomes disconnected abstracts
Several source summaries are accurate but no relationships or disagreements are explained.
Inspect: organize around the user's questions and identify what each source supports, limits, or changes. Do not invent an apparent consensus.

## Readability hides overconfidence
English parentheses disappear, but 'safety is guaranteed' remains after three successful commands.
Inspect: distinguish configured boundary, observed trials, untested controls, and permitted damaging actions. Style and truth require separate checks.
