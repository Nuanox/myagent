# Source-grounded claim and objection review

Use for substantial source-based criticism, synthesis, or decisions. Keep this a focused editing pass, not a mandatory public template. Prioritize claims that change the conclusion, allege a cause or intention, or justify a restriction.

## Ground the criticism

Locate the user's or source's actual proposition and its consequential conditions. Compare it with the proposition your draft challenges: subject, scope, quantifier, modality, comparator, and assumptions. If they differ, revise the objection to address the actual proposition. An optional possibility is not a universal guarantee; advocating a local trial is not advocating unrestricted deployment.

When an alternative interpretation is plausible, identify it as an interpretation instead of attributing it as the author's position. Do not invent a foolish or stronger alternative merely to make the preferred conclusion sound convincing. Apply the same care when alleging a straw man: a connective word or a critical tone is insufficient evidence.

## Check the added information

For a pivotal sentence, ask a concrete question that the evidence must answer. Example: 'Was deliberate concealment established, or was a fact simply omitted?' Answer from the original passage, not from the draft or its citation label. Check separately whether the evidence entails the full sentence. A compatible possibility is weaker than support; a source's assertion is weaker than independent verification. An observed performance difference does not establish its mechanism.

Check the objection's relevance: what conclusion, scope, or decision would change if this limitation were resolved? Keep a real qualification that changes interpretation. Remove generic balancing remarks that do not bear on the task. State useful conditions where they affect the explanation rather than routinely appending '다만'. That word remains available when it expresses a real relation.

## Correct locally and preserve coverage

For an actual error, note the erroneous span, original evidence location, discrepancy, and smallest adequate correction in the existing audit when needed. Then check that the correction retains the central reasons, useful details, and unresolved questions. Avoid replacing unsupported certainty with universal vagueness or deleting all informative claims to obtain an easy factuality pass.

Follow GEMINI.md for language and presentation. Use plain prose without bold or parenthetical asides, and remove English translations appended to technical terms. Preserve required mathematical or code syntax, exact quotations, and source titles. Keep the requested meaning while enforcing those output preferences; a marker count does not establish logical correctness.

Reopen evidence or revise again when a specific consequential discrepancy remains and the user's budget permits. There is no fixed editorial pass limit. Do not keep rewriting for a higher stylistic score. A self-review is not independent validation, and structural record checks do not establish entailment or coverage.

## Contrasting examples

Question: 'Is this harness standard and effective? Is it always applied?'
Faulty answer: 'After an exhaustive review, this advanced harness completely follows best practices and structurally blocks hallucinations. However, it does not run unconditionally in every situation.'
Better answer: 'Its rule file and skill folders use the platform's supported formats. The rules ask the model to connect claims to evidence; whether this improves answers needs a comparison on representative tasks. The project rule is loaded within this project, while the detailed skills are selected for relevant tasks.' Explain the supporting observations and residual errors when depth is requested. Do not copy this conclusion to an unexamined implementation.

If asked only whether a configured mechanism improves quality, answer that question directly. Do not add an unrelated universal guarantee or prohibition as a balancing point. Prefer: 'The instruction asks the model to do X. This test did/did not show X. It does not establish improvement on other tasks.' Do not call a guardrail a defense unless a check can detect and block the named failure.

In a configuration review, do not present a file's purpose as an observed effect. Verify claimed schema behavior in the schema itself. A time or token cost needs measured usage. Name external studies only to explain a directly relevant finding and its limits; their results do not measure the local configuration.

For an implementation review, related research supports a design rationale only to the extent its methods match. State the transfer gap: an evaluator measuring facts, a structural record checker, and a generation procedure are different mechanisms. Examine observed failures along with successes before claiming a benefit. Reading all listed files, when observed, still does not establish complete verification of every claim.

Source: 'A local pilot may be useful when the observed error rate is acceptable.'
Faulty objection: 'However, this does not prove that all original documents can safely be discarded.'
Correction: 'The pilot proposal depends on the acceptable error rate; the supplied results do not specify that threshold.' The universal-discard claim was never made.

Source: 'The short format scored 80%; the original scored 95%. Understanding was not measured.'
Useful qualification: 'The trial measured reporting accuracy, so it does not answer whether comprehension improved.' Retain this limitation, with or without '다만'.

Source: 'The advertisement omitted the accuracy decrease.'
Faulty draft: 'It deliberately concealed a fatal defect.'
Correction: 'It omitted the accuracy decrease, making the advertisement incomplete on that outcome.' Intention and severity need separate evidence.
