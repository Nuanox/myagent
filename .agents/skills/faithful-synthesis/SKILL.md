# Faithful synthesis

Follow the project GEMINI.md for reply language, register, interpretation of user words, and presentation. This skill supplies synthesis procedures; it does not relax the prose-format requirements.

Use the shared routing criteria in GEMINI.md. Read this workflow before interpreting source claims or drafting a substantive source-based answer. A source-only comparison or recommendation stays within the supplied evidence. If authorized investigation beyond the specified sources is needed, read deep-research before investigating, then return to this workflow for the explanation.

Reduce the reader's effort without removing what they need to understand the conclusion. Determine whether the user asks for a source summary, cross-source synthesis, instruction-compliance review, independent correctness review, or several deliverables. Provide all requested deliverables; do not substitute your evaluation for the source's explanation.

When answering an adoption or decision question, separate empirical findings from your decision criterion. For a requested recommendation, choose the best-supported option for the user's criterion. If a missing criterion would change the choice, ask or state an explicit working assumption; explain the relevant tradeoff without imposing a universal prohibition or exclusive permitted use. 'Not established by these studies' and 'should be deferred under an accuracy-first criterion' are different conclusions. State any criterion you choose and its limits. Put the connected answer and reasons in the report body; source-by-source details belong in supporting records when they interrupt the explanation.

Example of proportionate decision prose: 'The supplied trials show a time/accuracy tradeoff under their tested conditions. They do not establish suitability for an unspecified new task. If the observed error rate exceeds the user's tolerance, these findings favor retaining the original; if that loss is acceptable, a local pilot could test whether the time benefit persists. Evidence for another format is promising on the measured outcome, but its unmeasured costs remain open.' Adapt to actual evidence, not these words. Never infer that greater complexity causes a larger loss by comparing unrelated studies, or add applications outside a source-only task as though studied.

## Reconstruct before compressing
Read relevant source sections, including qualifications and objections. Identify the central question, what is asserted, the evidence or argument supporting it, consequential assumptions, and open decisions. Track relationships: supports, requires, limits, contrasts, precedes, or causes only when warranted. A source may state a causal claim that is not independently established; preserve that distinction.

For long documents locate evidence distributed across sections. Retrieve prerequisites, definitions, counterarguments, and boundary conditions along with apparently relevant conclusions. Use concise evidence notes with original locators; revisit original passages for decisive statements. Context-window size and keyword similarity do not establish complete coverage.

For multiple sources organize around questions and explanations, not one miniature abstract per source. Distinguish common evidence from independent evidence; explain disagreement rather than flattening it into consensus. Keep the source's argument separate from your corrections or new synthesis. If a source's reasoning has a gap, present it honestly instead of silently repairing it.

## Write for understanding

For sustained work, outline the question and necessary logical links before writing. Compose from original evidence and that outline, not concatenated AI summaries. Draft and check the substance first, then edit the prose under GEMINI.md. Edit the sentence's function and connection to its neighbors rather than substituting milder synonyms. Keep decisive quantities, comparators, qualifications, and unresolved questions; revisit the evidence when a proposed rewrite changes the claim. Revisit either when a consequential error remains; no fixed number of editing passes applies. The reader should understand the answer without opening a compliance checklist.

For ordinary explanatory prose, use the paragraphs needed to explain the answer. Match depth to the question rather than targeting a paragraph count. Keep relevant premises and the reason linking them beside the conclusion. In a source-only task, add no speculative applications as though studied.

Use the short Korean examples in GEMINI.md as models of plain explanation. Adapt them to the actual evidence and question. Preserve useful caveats, names, mathematics, and requested genres. Do not rewrite quotations to change their meaning or make their author sound more cautious.
Use connected paragraphs in the reply language and register specified by GEMINI.md. Give necessary context before relying on an unfamiliar term. Show why a result follows and what restricts it. Adapt the organization: premises and conclusions for arguments, sequence and causality for narratives, dependencies for procedures, choices and tradeoffs for exploratory proposals. A single universal template is not required.

Retain details whose loss changes interpretation: negation, domain, timeframe, denominator, comparator, assumptions, uncertainty, exceptions, and modal force. Compress repetition and incidental examples first. 'May' cannot become 'does'; 'allowed' cannot become 'required'; a selected model cannot become the only possible model. Do not hide limits in parentheses.

Avoid English glosses, internal tool identifiers, decorative headings, praise, or compressed fragments that force the user to reconstruct the argument. Use exact names and mathematical notation when needed. Tables support comparisons; they do not replace a requested explanation. Keep the source, tested task, timing, outcome definition, and calculation basis attached to each numerical comparison. Do not combine unlike outcomes into one range or replace the measured construct with a more impressive name. An executive overview may precede a substantive account when useful, but each part must add something distinct. Explain the reasoning behind a comparison table rather than restating its rows. Keep the substantive account available when depth is requested.

## Validate in both directions

For substantive criticism or a consequential recommendation, use [claim-review.md](references/claim-review.md): compare the actual proposition and its scope to the claim being challenged, then check support and decision relevance. Review only the consequential passages; do not impose a checklist or extra files on every sentence. For prose formatting, enforce GEMINI.md: no bold or parenthetical asides in your own prose. A formatting violation and a reasoning error are different findings; a word such as '다만' alone does not establish a strawman. Preserve useful qualifications and information while correcting the specific error.
From report to source: check decisive claims, quantifiers, numbers, causal links, and adjacent citations against the actual evidence. From source to report: check whether omitted assumptions, exceptions, competing results, or user-relevant questions would change the reader's understanding. Citation support does not measure omitted content.

Ask whether the reader can explain what is claimed, why, under what conditions, and what remains unresolved. Restore missing logical links. Do not invent links just to improve flow. Check evidential strength in the connecting sentences and the conclusion, not only in each source description. If a draft first asserts a cause and later admits that cause is unverified, narrow the earlier assertion. Unmeasured or unresolved domains are unknown, not demonstrated failures. If the requested length cannot carry every detail, retain the most consequential qualifications and clearly limit scope.

For compliance reviews compare the actual content to substantive and negative instructions, including prohibited work inside a stage. For correctness reviews check both favorable and adverse judgments; another premise may repair an apparent gap. Use concrete evidence before any overall verdict. A checked computation is distinct from a valid premise, complete proof, or satisfactory model.

Before submission review the strongest adverse statements, not just numerical transcription. Distinguish insufficient evidence for adoption from evidence of impossibility; an unreported method from a method known not to have been used; observed differences from statistically established effects; omitted information from intentional concealment; lower accuracy from a demonstrated causal mechanism. A source's suggestion to consider a limited use does not establish that all other uses are prohibited. Explain a judgment by linking it to the observed contrast and design, without adding an untested mechanism. Check whether words such as necessary, optimal, or always assert a condition that the study actually compared. A useful recommendation may remain a conditional judgment; an illustrative schedule must not become a tested optimum. Identify exactly which source supports each strengthening word. If none does, narrow the statement and preserve the open decision. An audit must record checks actually performed and residual gaps rather than declaring its own rigor.

Use [failure-cases.md](references/failure-cases.md) when reviewing unfamiliar long reasoning or evaluating a changed workflow. It provides known pitfalls, not assertions about a new source. Keep research audit notes concise and factual. A self-check is not independent verification.
